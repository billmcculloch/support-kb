  • parser. The shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so day and month swap. This is the bug #4471 names.
  • hint. The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field and then reads the date day first. People do what the hint says, so the date swaps. The parser fix does not touch this form.
  • one-day move. The date moves by one day, not by a swap: Tuesday becomes Monday, Lisbon and Toronto see different days, an invite arrives before its booking. This looks like a time-zone bug. The desk had already read these reports as one-day moves (desk-notes.json), but the tenant rule filed them under parser.

Where each one stands

From the engineering update that closed #4471 on 5 October:

  • parser: fixed in “fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales”. Ticket closed.
  • hint: won’t fix, because the booking-v3 form is retired. Ticket closed as not planned.
  • one-day move: not in the update, and the parser fix does not change a one-day shift. Still open. New one-day reports go on its ticket.

Why they were merged

Decision 0001 merges reports on subject line alone. All three problems produce the same subjects (“Date is wrong”, “Wrong day on the invite”), so one ticket took them all. Intake tags do not separate them either: they come from the reporter’s browser, and the cause comes from the tenant’s form and locale.
